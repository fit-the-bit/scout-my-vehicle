How to connect your daily Google Sheet (Step-by-step)

Download Template

Click the button above to download the pre-formatted .csv template containing Haldwani & Rudrapur columns.

Import to Sheets

Go to sheets.new, then click File → Import → Upload and choose the template CSV.

3
Share Publicly (View)

In your Google Sheet, click Share (top right) → Change General Access to "Anyone with the link can view".

4
Paste & Sync Daily

Paste your Google Sheet link above and hit Sync Stock Now whenever dealerships provide new stock numbers!
