About ScoutMyVehicle

The Smarter, Honest Way to Discover Your Next Car

ScoutMyVehicle is a new-car discovery and dealer-referral service, built to help car buyers easily identify their preferred vehicles and connect with authorized multi-brand dealerships.

What ScoutMyVehicle Is

Your independent automotive discovery & referral platform

ScoutMyVehicle is a new-car discovery and dealer-referral service designed to simplify the vehicle search process for prospective car buyers. We assist buyers across 9 major manufacturers—including Tata Motors, Mahindra, Hyundai, Kia, Toyota, Maruti Suzuki (Arena & Nexa), Honda, Volkswagen, and Nissan.

Important Operational Notice: ScoutMyVehicle does not currently own or maintain a general inventory of vehicles for sale. Vehicle availability is subject to confirmation by the relevant dealership.

Rather than calling individual showrooms repeatedly or traveling across dealerships to check vehicle allocations, buyers can explore detailed model catalogues, estimate on-road pricing and EMIs, and request availability checks. Once a matching vehicle is identified, we introduce you directly to the authorized dealership to complete your booking, quotation, and purchase.

Why We Started It

Buying a new car has often been an opaque and time-consuming process. Buyers frequently spend weekends visiting distant showrooms just to inquire whether their desired variant and preferred colour can be delivered within their required timeline.

We started ScoutMyVehicle to make car discovery convenient, efficient, and transparent. We gather your vehicle requirements, check availability with relevant authorized dealerships, and connect you with the appropriate showroom team.

The Problems We Solve

  • ✓ Multi-Brand Comparison: Explore variants, transmissions, and fuel options across top brands in one clean catalogue.
  • ✓ No Customer Commission: We do not charge customers a purchase commission for connecting them with an authorized dealer.
  • ✓ Dealer Availability Check: One inquiry enables us to verify availability across relevant authorized dealerships.
  • ✓ Direct Dealer Connection: Once confirmed, talk and purchase directly from the authorized dealer sales desk.

1 Platform Role & Referral Model

Important Notice on Platform Operations:

ScoutMyVehicle operates solely as an independent new-car discovery and dealer-referral platform. ScoutMyVehicle is NOT an automobile dealer, manufacturer, authorized franchisee, or direct seller of motor vehicles.

By accessing our website or submitting an enquiry, you acknowledge and agree that ScoutMyVehicle provides an informational service connecting prospective car buyers with authorized regional dealerships. Submitting an enquiry does not constitute a vehicle booking, deposit, or purchase contract, and does not guarantee vehicle availability or allocation. We do not participate in, control, or execute the actual retail sale, invoicing, vehicle registration, or physical delivery of any automobile.

2 Vehicle Availability Subject to Dealership Confirmation

All vehicle listings, variant specifications, and model metrics presented on ScoutMyVehicle represent model catalogue reference information.

Vehicle availability changes dynamically. All availability is subject to physical verification and written confirmation by the respective authorized dealership at the time of your formal booking. ScoutMyVehicle cannot and does not guarantee that a specific chassis, colour, or variant will remain available prior to formal booking receipt issuance by the dealership.

3 Prices, Taxes, and Offers Are Subject to Change

Ex-showroom prices, estimated on-road prices, regional road taxes (RTO), registration fees, TCS, FASTag charges, insurance premiums, and consumer schemes displayed on ScoutMyVehicle are indicative estimations intended for budgeting guidance.

Automobile manufacturers (OEMs) and authorized dealerships reserve the unilateral right to revise vehicle prices, specifications, color palettes, and promotional discounts without prior notice. The final payable on-road amount is determined strictly by the authorized dealership's official quotation and tax invoice prevailing on the date of invoicing.

4 ScoutMyVehicle Is Not the Seller

ScoutMyVehicle does not own, manufacture, hold title to, lease, or take physical custody of any motor vehicles. No contract of sale, purchase agreement, or warranty contract is formed between the customer and ScoutMyVehicle.

We do not accept vehicle purchase payments, booking deposits, or registration charges on behalf of dealerships. All commercial payments must be executed directly between the buyer and the authorized dealership through official banking channels.

5 Sole Responsibility of Authorized Dealerships

The authorized manufacturer dealership selected for your vehicle purchase is solely and exclusively responsible for:

  • Issuing the formal, legally binding on-road price quotation and commercial tax invoice.
  • Receiving official booking amounts, issuing receipts, and administering cancellation or refund policies.
  • Vehicle Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI), scheduling delivery dates, and physical vehicle handover.
  • Processing registration documents with the Regional Transport Office (RTO) and affixing High Security Registration Plates (HSRP).
  • Honouring manufacturer warranties, extended warranty coverage, road-side assistance, and scheduled service intervals.

ScoutMyVehicle accepts no legal liability for any dealership delivery delays, dealer cancellation disputes, mechanical vehicle defects, or registration issues arising between the buyer and the dealership.
