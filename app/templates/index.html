What ScoutMyVehicle Is

Your independent automotive intelligence partner

ScoutMyVehicle is an independent showroom inventory tracker and buyer-advocacy platform designed specifically for smart car buyers. We track verified floor availability, in-transit allocations, and ready stock across authorized dealership networks spanning 9 major manufacturers—including Tata Motors, Mahindra, Hyundai, Kia, Toyota, Maruti Suzuki (Arena & Nexa), Honda, Volkswagen, and Nissan.

Rather than relying on outdated dealer lists or driving across distant showrooms, buyers can check live stock availability, calculate accurate on-road pricing, and connect directly with showroom decision-makers on WhatsApp with zero middlemen markup.

Why We Started It

Buying a car has traditionally been a stressful, fragmented ordeal. Buyers often spent whole weekends shuttling back and forth between showrooms just to find out whether their preferred variant and colour was physically available.

Showrooms frequently quoted arbitrary 4-to-6-month waiting periods, pushed unwanted accessories worth tens of thousands of rupees, or urged buyers to travel long distances. We started ScoutMyVehicle to end the guesswork and give buyers transparent, verified information right from their phone.

Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 • We respect your privacy and never sell customer data to telemarketers.

1 What Customer Information We Collect

When you use ScoutMyVehicle to search for vehicles, request floor availability, or inquire about financing, we may collect the following details:

Personal Contact Details
Full Name, Mobile / WhatsApp Phone Number, Email Address (optional), and Resident City or District.
Vehicle Preferences
Chosen manufacturer, car model, specific variant, fuel type, transmission, exterior colour, and intended buying timeline.
Finance & Exchange Choices
Whether you need auto loan assistance, preferred bank (SBI, PNB, HDFC, Chola Mandlam), and current vehicle details if requesting exchange valuation.
Technical Log Data
Standard internet log data, IP address, device type, and page access timestamps used strictly for platform security and bot prevention.

2 Why We Collect Phone Number, Name, Location & Car Preferences

  • Verify Genuine Inquiries: Collecting verified phone numbers prevents automated bots, fake leads, and spam requests from disrupting dealership inventory allocations.
  • Showroom Stock Matching: Dealership inventory managers require exact vehicle, variant, fuel, and colour specifications to cross-reference their DMS (Dealer Management System) for chassis numbers and factory dispatch slots.
  • Regional Routing: Your location allows us to match you with the nearest authorized showroom in your locality so you receive accurate RTO tax calculations and prompt local service.
  • Instant Direct Communication: Having your WhatsApp and phone number enables a seamless one-click bridge between you and the dealership sales manager.

3 How Information is Used

Your information is used strictly to fulfill your requested automotive services:

  • Verifying physical floor inventory and booking queues with the respective authorized dealership.
  • Providing customized on-road cost breakdowns including regional road taxes and insurance quotes.
  • Forwarding approved bank financing inquiries to regional auto loan specialists at partner banks.
  • Generating pre-formatted WhatsApp chat links so you can communicate directly with showroom staff.
  • Maintaining service logs and preventing fraudulent activity.

4 Information Sharing with Dealerships & Third Parties

Strictly Limited Dealership Sharing:

We share your inquiry details only with the authorized dealership that represents the specific car brand you enquired about (e.g., if you inquire about a Tata Nexon, details are forwarded exclusively to the authorized Tata dealership).

We DO NOT sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal details to third-party telemarketing networks, insurance cold-callers, or generic data brokers. You will not receive unsolicited spam through our service.

5 Data Security & Protection

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. All web communications are encrypted via SSL/TLS (HTTPS). Access to customer inquiry databases is strictly restricted to authenticated administrators.

1 Platform Role & Lead-Generation Model

Important Notice on Platform Operations:

ScoutMyVehicle operates solely as an independent automotive discovery, information tracking, and lead-referral platform. ScoutMyVehicle is NOT an automobile dealer, broker, authorized franchisee, or direct seller of motor vehicles.

By accessing our website or submitting an inquiry, you acknowledge and agree that ScoutMyVehicle provides an informational bridge connecting prospective car buyers with authorized regional dealerships. We do not participate in, control, or execute the actual retail sale, invoicing, vehicle registration, or physical delivery of any automobile.

2 Vehicle Availability Subject to Showroom Confirmation

All vehicle listings, ready-stock tallies, in-transit allocation estimates, and variant availability metrics presented on ScoutMyVehicle are based on periodic inventory feeds provided by dealerships.

Vehicle stock rotates rapidly due to retail demand. All availability is subject to physical verification and written confirmation by the respective authorized dealership at the time of your formal booking. ScoutMyVehicle cannot and does not guarantee that a specific chassis, colour, or variant will remain available prior to formal booking receipt issuance by the dealership.

3 Prices, Taxes, and Offers Are Subject to Change

Ex-showroom prices, estimated on-road prices, regional road taxes (RTO), registration fees, TCS, FASTag charges, insurance premiums, and festive consumer schemes displayed on ScoutMyVehicle are indicative estimations intended for budgeting guidance.

Automobile manufacturers (OEMs) and authorized dealerships reserve the unilateral right to revise vehicle prices, specifications, color palettes, and promotional discounts without prior notice. The final payable on-road amount is determined strictly by the authorized dealership's official quotation and tax invoice prevailing on the date of invoicing.

4 ScoutMyVehicle Is Not the Seller

ScoutMyVehicle does not own, manufacture, hold title to, lease, or take physical custody of any motor vehicles. No contract of sale, purchase agreement, or warranty contract is formed between the customer and ScoutMyVehicle.

We do not accept vehicle purchase payments, booking deposits, or registration charges on behalf of dealerships. All commercial payments must be executed directly between the buyer and the authorized dealership through official banking channels.

5 Sole Responsibility of Authorized Dealerships

The authorized manufacturer dealership selected for your vehicle purchase is solely and exclusively responsible for:

  • Issuing the formal, legally binding on-road price quotation and commercial tax invoice.
  • Receiving official booking amounts, issuing receipts, and administering cancellation or refund policies.
  • Vehicle Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI), scheduling delivery dates, and physical vehicle handover.
  • Processing registration documents with the Regional Transport Office (RTO) and affixing High Security Registration Plates (HSRP).
  • Honouring manufacturer warranties, extended warranty coverage, road-side assistance, and scheduled service intervals.

ScoutMyVehicle accepts no legal liability for any showroom delivery delays, dealer cancellation disputes, mechanical vehicle defects, or registration issues arising between the buyer and the dealership.

  • No Automated Scraping: Automated scraping, crawling, bots, reverse engineering, or data extraction of vehicle inventory feeds, prices, or dealer lists is expressly forbidden without written consent.
